The progression of that internalization process is the work of the Holy Spirit in me, and the processes described are just ways to open myself up to that work. The Affective Taxonomy, which describes the observable component of that progress, is foundational to this volume.

Connect-with-Self is the precondition for Connect-with-Others, which is the precondition for the deeper reaches of Connect-with-God and Connect-with-Mission.
