Vol 1 is the basic development of laws or relationships I see in scripture, along with my personal assessment of certainty. This volume points to a draft Table of Periodic Elements (like the one for chemical elements) that offers an organizing principle for identifying gaps and predicting undiscovered (at least by me) additional laws.

If I have truly grasped a law of the Spirit, my doing will change. Otherwise, I have only memorized it, which is different. If I am not changed by God's word, then I haven't yet heard God's word.
