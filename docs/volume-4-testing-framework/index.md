# Volume 4: Testing Framework

## How to read this volume

Volume 4 is the research-design arm of the Intentional Journey of the Heart. Where Volumes 1–3 develop the *claims* — what the spiritual life appears to operate by — and Volume 5 provides the formation documents and protocols that put those claims into practice, Volume 4 asks the next question: **how would we know if the claims are actually true?** It proposes the instruments, the small-group and individual protocols, and the multi-site research design that could put the LotS framework to a genuine test.

A first-time reader will get the most out of this volume if they have already read, or have on hand:

- **V1.Exp1 — *How to Get Faith*** for the Word→Hearing→Faith chain (Rom. 10:17), which the testing claims keep returning to.
- **V2.Exp7 — *Hearing with Understanding*** for the PROAPT model (Pray / Read / Observe / Apply / Pray / Tell). PROAPT is the small-group practice the entire testing program observes; if you have not met it before, that chapter introduces it.
- **V2.Exp8 + V2.Exp8B** for the *container* and *session liturgy* claims that ground the small-group testing unit.
- **Vol 5 Formation Documents — TA (Theological Anthropology) and HFT (Heart Formation Theology)** for the Image of God anthropology and the 4Rs (Reason, Rulership, Relationship, Righteousness) drawing on Kilner (2015). These are PDFs in [Vol 5 source-pdfs](../volume-5-references/index.md).
- **Vol 5 Formation Document — FC (Formation Companion)** for the Companion-role formation theology that §4e operationalizes as facilitator criteria.
- **Vol 6 Part 1 — *Governance Model*** for the **claim registry** and the **annual calibration cycle**. Every testable hypothesis in Vol 4 inherits its confidence rating from a registered Vol 1 or 2 claim; the calibration cycle is where Vol 4's data formally adjusts that confidence up or down.

The reading order within Vol 4 itself matters less than the reading order from Vol 1–3 into Vol 4. Within Vol 4, Sections 1 and 2 set the epistemological frame; Sections 3 and 4 describe what to measure (individual and small-group); Section 5 puts it together into a multi-site research program. A reader who only wants the individual-level protocol can read §1 → §3; a reader designing for a small group should read §1 → §2 → §4; a reader thinking about a multi-site study reads everything in order.

## Glossary

### LotS claim codes used in this volume

The LotS-* codes refer to claims registered in Vols 1–2 (the canonical registry lives in [Vol 6 Part 1 §2 — The Claim Registry](../volume-6-governance/part-1-governance-model.md#2-the-claim-registry)). The codes used in Vol 4 are listed below with the working statement each code references. *If a code's exact wording diverges from the Vol 6 registry, the registry is authoritative.*

| Code | Working statement |
|---|---|
| **LotS-F1** | Faith arrives through the Rom. 10:17 mechanism (Word → Hearing → Faith) |
| **LotS-F2** | Faith is primarily affective, not cognitive |
| **LotS-F3** | Trials are not interruptions to faith formation but the primary mechanism for stage transitions (Jas. 1:2–3) |
| **LotS-F4** | Community is essential to faith formation (the Rom. 10 chain requires sending, preaching, hearing) |
| **LotS-H1** | The heart is the affective center through which all formation passes |
| **LotS-H2** | Unrepentant sin progressively hardens the heart |
| **LotS-H3** | Heart formation is progressive and follows the five stages of the Affective Taxonomy (Receiving → Responding → Valuing → Organization → Characterization) |
| **LotS-H4** | Reflective bible study (PROAPT model) is the primary discipline that advances heart formation at every stage |
| **LotS-I2** | Spiritual formation is growth in reflection of the image of God, measurable through the 4Rs |
| **LotS-I3** | Image of God growth is observable in the four R-dimensions individually (Reason, Rulership, Relationship, Righteousness) |
| **LotS-W1** | Wisdom development is activated by affective desire, not cognitive accumulation alone |
| **LotS-SF1 / LotS-SF2** | Spiritual force and spiritual energy claims (the hardest in the LotS framework to operationalize) |

### Instruments used in the testing protocols

The Vol 4 testing protocol is built around a **Pilot Ready core** of three individual and three group instruments, designed so that a real facilitator in a real church can run the protocol without the assessment load competing with their presence in the room. Eight additional instruments from the earlier full-suite design are preserved as **research-grade extensions** in the [Vol 4 Appendix](appendix-research-extensions.md); they remain available for studies wanting deeper instrumentation in specific dimensions, with a known cost in facilitator and participant load.

**Pilot Ready core instruments**

| Code | Name | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| **SIP-Individual** | Stage Integration Profile (individual) | Profile across the five AT stages with closure check, scored as a forced-choice 100-point distribution across six personal formation dimensions, current and preferred passes |
| **NRP** | Narrative Reflection Protocol | Monthly first-person journal across five structured prompts (hearing the word, trial response, heart change, Image of God 4R growth, SIP closure-flag follow-up when triggered) |
| **EWI** | External Witness Interview | Independent observer (family member, close friend, coworker) interviewed at baseline and Month 12 |
| **Group SIP** | Stage Integration Profile (group culture) | Group's collective culture profile across the five AT stages with closure check, six group-culture dimensions, member-aggregated with facilitator's separate completion as a diagnostic comparison |
| **Tell-Rate count** | Weekly single-question facilitator check | Percentage of participants completing the PROAPT Tell step (naming + report-back) — the Romans 10 chain operating at the group level |
| **Facilitator SIP** | SIP-Individual completed by the facilitator on themselves | Facilitator's own integration profile as an independent variable in group-outcome analysis; the closure-flag surfaces the integrated-vs-closed Stage 4 facilitator pattern |

**Other construct used at the group level**

| Code | Name | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| **GATS** | Group Affective Taxonomy Stage | Working construct: the stage characterizing the group's *shared culture* — not the average of individual stages. Operationally surfaced through the Group SIP profile. The [§4b GATS working definition](section-4-small-group-spiritual-formation-testing.md#4b-open-trail-ot-6-can-a-group-move-through-the-affective-taxonomy) names a closure shadow rating that Group SIP measures directly. |

**Research-grade extensions** (canonical descriptions in the [Vol 4 Appendix](appendix-research-extensions.md))

PSFA (Personal Spiritual Formation Assessment), STI-A (Scripture Trust Index — Adapted), SDFL (Spiritual Disciplines Frequency Log), GSFP (Group Spiritual Formation Profile), SOP (Session Observation Protocol), CSFA (Collective Spiritual Formation Assessment), GCS (Group Cohesion Scale), Facilitator PSFA, OT-5 Supplementary Interview. Each appendix entry includes a "when to add this instrument" guidance paragraph naming the specific research question or pastoral need it serves.

### Other recurring terms

- **Affective Taxonomy** — the five-stage developmental model (Receiving / Responding / Valuing / Organization / Characterization) used throughout the volume. Originates in Krathwohl's 1964 taxonomy of educational objectives and is developed for spiritual formation by Bramer (see Vol 5 references). The Vol 4 working version is summarized in the Quick Reference callout in [§3](section-3-individual-spiritual-formation-testing-protocol.md).
- **PROAPT** — the six-step reflective bible-study practice (Pray / Read / Observe / Apply / Pray / Tell). The Vol 2 home is [V2.Exp7 *Hearing with Understanding*](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/exploration-07-hearing-with-understanding.md); the operational protocol form is [A28 Heart Bible Study with PROAPT](../volume-5-references/a28-heart-bible-study-with-proapt.md) in Vol 5.
- **4Rs** — Reason, Rulership, Relationship, Righteousness. The four dimensions of Image-of-God reflection drawn from Kilner (2015) and developed across the Vol 5 Formation Documents (TA, HFT).
- **MOVE study** — Hawkins & Parkinson (2011); the empirical study at Willow Creek that identified four distinguishable growth stages with three transition movements, and the finding that reflective bible study is the one discipline present at every stage.
- **NCD** — Christian Schwarz's *Natural Church Development*; Vol 4 borrows two specific things from NCD: the *minimum-factor diagnostic logic* (find the rate-limiting variable, intervene there first) and the *3 Colors of Community* communal instrument as a convergent-validity check for the GSFP.
