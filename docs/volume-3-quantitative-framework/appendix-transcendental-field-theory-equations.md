**What's the difference?**

- **Reconstructed *(recommended for reading)*** — Vision-OCR transcription of the original PDF, with right-edge truncations patched against parallel research notes (each patch annotated inline so it stays traceable). Properly typeset with LaTeX, ~315 KB, 69 pages.
- **Faithful transcription** — Same vision-OCR transcription as the reconstructed edition, but **unedited**. Preserves exactly what was visible in the original screenshots — including any truncations at the screenshot edges — as a forensic record. ~311 KB, 69 pages.
- **Original source** — The original PDF as authored: primarily MathJax-rendered equation screenshots assembled into a Word document and exported. Visually correct but heavy (~13 MB, 125 pages) and not text-searchable; some equations are clipped at the page edge in the source itself.
