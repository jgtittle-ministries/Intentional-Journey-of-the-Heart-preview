# Fourth Exploration: Spiritual Distance — Kinematics Before Dynamics


![](images/image-007.jpeg)

## Why We Need Distance Before Force
Newton built dynamics on top of Galileo’s kinematics. You cannot define force (F = mA) until you have defined acceleration, and you cannot define acceleration until you have defined velocity, and you cannot define velocity until you have defined distance and time. The Vol 3 program requires the same sequence. Spiritual force requires spiritual acceleration, which requires spiritual velocity, which requires spiritual distance.

This leaves spiritual time open to discussion and exploration. The concept that the world of the Spirit needs only one extra dimension, beyond our recognized four, to understand the spiritual math is a superset of our natural laws. Not sure how this extends to time in the world of the Spirit.

## Distance as Degree of Entanglement
The most interesting concept in the Vol 3 draft notes is the quantum entanglement analogy for spiritual distance. In quantum mechanics, entanglement is a form of correlation that is stronger than classical correlation and that does not diminish with physical distance. Two entangled particles, separated by any physical distance, respond to each other instantaneously. The analogy I want to propose: spiritual closeness to God is not well-described by a spatial metaphor (how far away is He?) but by an entanglement metaphor (how deeply correlated are my states with His?).

***John 17:21 (ESV)***

*“...that they may all be one, just as you, Father, are in me, and I in you, that they also may be in us, so that the world may believe that you have sent me.”*

The perichoretic unity of the Trinity — mutual indwelling, not spatial proximity — is the model for what spiritual closeness ultimately means. The goal of the spiritual journey is not to get spatially closer to a distant God but to become increasingly entangled with Him: increasingly correlated in will, in perception, in response, in action. Zero spiritual distance is not nearness in space but the kind of unity Jesus describes in John 17 — being in each other as the Father and Son are in each other.

**TFT CONNECTION****:  ***TFT** locates this precisely in the transcendental manifold: spiritual distance is position in the (Truth, Goodness, Beauty) configuration space relative to the Glory attractor. The attractor is the global maximum at which all three fields reach their maximum values simultaneously. Moving toward the attractor — increasing in Truth, Goodness, and Beauty together — is what I am **calling spiritual distance reduction. The manifold’s curvature (determined by the coupling parameters α, β, γ) means the path is not a straight line: increasing in Truth without simultaneously growing in Goodness and Beauty pulls the trajectory away from the attractor, not toward it.*

## Three Dimensions of Spiritual Distance
Building on both the REVEAL framework and TFT’s configuration space, I am proposing that spiritual distance has at least three semi-independent dimensions, corresponding to TFT’s three primary coordinate axes:

- Truth-distance: How accurately does my current understanding of God, self, and the world correspond to reality as God sees it? The renewal of the mind (Rom. 12:2) is movement along this axis.
- Goodness-distance: How aligned is my will, motivation, and action with what is genuinely good? Sanctification (or spiritual formation) is movement along this axis.
- Beauty-distance: How deeply am I oriented toward the genuine beauty of God — the attractiveness of His character, His presence, His ways — rather than to substitute beauties? Worship is movement along this axis.
These three are not independent: the coupling parameters (α, β, γ in TFT’s language) mean that movement along one axis tends to produce movement along the others. This is why genuine worship (Beauty-axis movement) produces moral motivation (Goodness-axis movement), not because they are the same thing, but because they are coupled. The suggestion is that a person who grows in genuine awe of God’s beauty tends to grow in desire for His goodness as well.

**HYPOTHESIS: Spiritual distance is a three-dimensional quantity in Truth-Goodness-Beauty configuration space, not a single scalar. Movement toward the Glory attractor requires simultaneous progress on all three axes; movement along one axis alone, while temporarily reducing distance in that dimension, does not reliably reduce overall spiritual distance if the other two axes are neglected.**

The coupling between axes is less precisely understood. This is a candidate for community testing using to be developed instruments to distinguish the three dimensions.*

## The Affective Taxonomy as a Measurement Protocol

![](images/image-005.jpeg)

The measurement problem — how to operationalize spiritual distance in a way that yields real data — has a more tractable partial solution than I initially recognized. My doctoral research in spiritual formation proposes the Affective Taxonomy (Krathwohl, Bloom, and Masia, 1964) as a defensible framework for setting objectives and measuring progress in the specific domain of trusting scripture, and also hearing and obeying God, which is arguably the central behavioral indicator of spiritual distance in this framework.

The five levels of the taxonomy (Receiving, Responding, Valuing, Organization, Characterization) map directly onto observable behavioral indicators that can be assessed both by self-report and by external witnesses. A seven-point Likert scale anchored at “no trust — this is just a collection of old fables” through to “complete trust and incorporation into a personal biblical worldview” provides the self-report instrument. Observed behavior change, reported by small group members and external witnesses in regular contact with the person, provides the external validation.

Three things make this particularly useful for the Vol 3 measurement program:

- It is operationalizable now, without waiting for the full three-axis spiritual distance measure to be developed. The Affective Taxonomy can be used to specifically measure the depth of trust in scripture, and the hearing/obeying internalization, which is the primary inflow variable in the faith stock.
- It has research precedent. The MOVE study (Hawkins and Parkinson, 2011) found that reflective Bible study is the one practice that appears as a catalyst at every stage and every movement between stages, which is precisely what the Affective Taxonomy would predict if trust in scripture is the master variable. The two frameworks are mutually confirming.
- It generates testable predictions. If spiritual distance decreases as Affective Taxonomy level increases, we should see measurable correlations between taxonomy stage and REVEAL stage across a community. If the taxonomy's Level 3 threshold (intrinsic valuing) corresponds to the MOVE transition from “Growing” to “Close to Christ,” that would be a significant result. This is runnable research.
**OPEN TRAIL: ***Can the Affective Taxonomy level (measured through the PROAPT-based small group assessment protocol described in Vol 2) be correlated with the REVEAL stage across a congregation? If the Level 3 threshold maps to the Growing-to-Close transition, this would provide the first quantitative bridge between the affective formation literature and the empirical church growth literature.*

The measurement-protocol reading of the AT requires the integration check that [Vol 4 §2: Reading the Affective Taxonomy as Integration, Not Replacement](../volume-4-testing-framework/section-2-testing-framework.md#reading-the-affective-taxonomy-as-integration-not-replacement) supplies and that [V2.Exp7](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/exploration-07-hearing-with-understanding.md#reading-these-levels-as-integration-not-replacement) develops in the pastoral register. *Measured* Affective Taxonomy level is a proxy for spiritual distance only when paired with the integration check — a person whose surface stage has risen while earlier-stage openness has collapsed (the Pharisee-as-stage-N-collapse pattern) would register as closer-to-Christ on the instrument while in fact being closed. Vol 4's Pilot Ready core operationalizes this directly through the **SIP closure-flag** rule (any stage scoring below 10 points on a profile dimension is flagged as collapse-suspect), and at the group level through the [§4b GATS closure shadow rating](../volume-4-testing-framework/section-4-small-group-spiritual-formation-testing.md#4b-open-trail-ot-6-can-a-group-move-through-the-affective-taxonomy); the Vol 3 measurement program inherits these.
