# Sixth Exploration (C): The Discernment-from-Outside-the-Frame Law

## A Discipline Operating at Five Altitudes
When I look across the protocols the corpus has developed — the Triangle facilitation move (A10), the Heart Prayer Time step 2 (*Father, where are You?*, B18), the Companion-as-witness discipline (the third paragraph of the refined Exploration 8 below), the source-reversal canonical series (eleven canonical instances now, across closing-affirmations, corporate-mission, embodied-resonance, blessing rounds, renunciation in Jesus's name, attending-and-choosing-in-active-communion, and other moves), and the refined V1.Exp2 (deliberate attending-and-choosing as the spirit's faculty of stepping outside System-1 default into deliberate processing) — I notice the same structural move operating at every altitude. The participant steps outside the immediate frame to receive what is true from outside it. The Companion holds the room without imposing the Companion's interpretation. The protocol locates agency at the Lord's level rather than at the participant's. The discipline is, in all five places, the same discipline.

The synthesis pass of May 13, 2026 named what I had not named at the canonical-claim altitude before: this is a structural law that governs across the altitudes. Discernment of what is true requires stepping outside the immediate frame to receive what is true from outside. The discipline is structurally constitutive of the corpus's hearing, healing, and formation work; without it, the work operates from inside whatever default frame the participant brings — which is precisely what the corpus's broader anthropology names as the System-1 / false-self / inside-the-knot starting position.

## What This Does at the Companion-Stance Level
The Companion's structural stance in any deployment of the Tool Map (Exploration 6) operates under this law. The Companion's discernment about *which tool to deploy and how to deploy it* requires the same from-outside-the-frame stance that the deployed tool will then help the participant practice. A Companion who selects a tool from inside the participant's frame — receiving the participant's account of the situation as the operational map — has already foreclosed on the work the tool was supposed to do. The Companion's discernment must operate from outside the participant's frame, in the Lord's, for the tool-selection itself to be sound.

This is not a new claim about agency — it is the same claim the corpus has carried in the source-reversal canonical series and in the V1.Exp2 anthropology, named explicitly at the level where it governs the operational discipline that makes the rest of the volume's work possible. The Christian tradition has names for this stance from its own resources: the Ignatian discipline of noticing, the Pauline *transformed-by-the-renewal-of-your-mind* discipline (Rom 12:2), the apophatic stance from the contemplative tradition.

**Proposed Law (Structural): Discernment of what is true requires stepping outside the immediate frame to receive what is true from outside. The discipline operates across multiple altitudes: at the facilitation-move altitude (A10 Triangle pulls the participant out of the scene to a discernment position); at the prayer-discipline altitude (B18 step 2 *****Father, where are You?***** steps the participant outside their own viewpoint to receive Christ's); at the Companion-as-witness altitude (Exploration 8 refined paragraph 3 holds the room without imposing the Companion's interpretation); at the source-reversal altitude (the Spirit-mediation framing ****locates agency at the Lord's level rather than at the participant's, across the eleven canonical instances of the source-reversal canonical series); at the theological-anthropology altitude (V1.Exp2 refined names the spirit's faculty of stepping outside System-1 default into deliberate attending-and-choosing). The discipline is structurally constitutive of the corpus's hearing, healing, and formation work; without it, the work operates from inside whatever default frame the participant brings. The Companion's structural stance in any deployment of the Tool Map operates under this law; the Companion's discernment about which tool to deploy and how to deploy it requires the same from-outside-the-frame stance that the deployed tool will then help the participant practice.**

**Certainty: Reasonably ****Inferred  ***The** discipline operates explicitly across five distinct altitudes of canonical practice; the Reasonably Inferred confidence reflects the operational pattern being well-established across the corpus's canonical practice. The synthesis-pass canonization (May 13, 2026) names the structural law that has been operating implicitly throughout; the canonization makes the law explicit without adding new substantive theological content. The law is theologically clean and convergent with multiple Christian-traditional disciplines (Ignatian noticing; the apophatic contemplative stance; the Pauline transformed-mind tradition).*
