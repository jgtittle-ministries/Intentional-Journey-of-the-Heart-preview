# Eighth Exploration (B): The Session Liturgy

## What This Volume Has Been Missing
For many years the corpus has named the pieces of the work — the four connects, the container, the clearings, the hearing-with-understanding, the community amplification, the corporate witness, the tool map, the import discipline — without naming explicitly the thing the pieces compose into. The thing is a *session*: a gathered time when a group meets to do this work together. The pieces are not items on a checklist; they are sequenced moves within one practice — one liturgy — and the integrity of the sequence is what makes the work the work. The May 2026 Phase 2 work on B16 (the Session Liturgy) and the synthesis pass that followed it surfaced this as a canonical claim in its own right, sitting as a compound-claim companion to Exploration 8's container claim.

The session has been operationally practiced in Band of Brothers and Fellowship of the Heart bodies since at least February 2004 (TGL's Band of Brothers Group Session Protocol is the earliest written form I know of) and in the *Field Manual: How to Start and Sustain a God-Focused Men's Small Group* (Tittle with Smith, 2008) as the practitioner-level operational depth. The 2004 protocol named what the practice has carried for decades, and the 2008 *Field Manual* gave the practitioner-level depth. What was missing until 2026 was the canonical-claim altitude statement of what the session *is*.

## The Components of a Session
A session has front-notes (orientation to what the work is and who is doing it); a formally-marked opening that establishes the container in the sense of Exploration 8; clearings that surface and address what would interfere with the work (PIES is the canonical clearing-method here — Exploration 8B's clearing sub-component is treated at depth in Vol 5 Part VI's B17 entry); the work itself, which the Companion has discerned for *this* gathering from the corpus's menu (a tool from Exploration 6's Map, a hearing exercise from Exploration 7, an Exploration 2/2A/2B/2C diagnostic move, a connection-work move from Exploration 5, the specific protocol the Companion has discerned the gathering needs); the formally-marked closing that integrates the work into life (the de-roling work, the closing affirmation, the marked release); and the teachings the body has learned to receive at the close.

Three points about this composition. **First**, the items are not optional checklist elements. They are sequenced moves within one practice. The opening without the work is empty ritual; the work without the closing dissipates; the closing without the opening floats. The integrity of the sequence is what makes the work the work. **Second**, the session is the Lord's. We are gathered in His name; the Spirit is among us; we are receiving the work the Lord is doing in the gathered body. The Companion's job is not to lead the work but to hold the liturgy that lets the Lord do the work He is doing. **Third**, the session liturgy is canonical home for community formation work in the corpus's practice — the place where the Tool Map (Exploration 6), the Container discipline (Exploration 8), the Community-as-Amplifier work (Exploration 9), and the Tool Import Discipline (Exploration 0) come together into one practice that is more than the sum of its parts.

The Christian tradition has names for this practice from its own resources — Acts 2:42–47's apostolic gathering pattern; Acts 13:1–3's ministering-to-the-Lord-and-fasting pattern; 1 Corinthians 14:26–33's orderly-gathering pattern; Matthew 18:18–20's gathered-in-the-name authority; Hebrews 10:23–25's assembling discipline; Acts 20:7–12's table-and-word pattern; Luke 24:13–35's Emmaus road master pattern. The Pietist conventicle lineage (Spener's *Pia Desideria*, 1675; Francke's *Hallesche** Berichte*; Wesley's class meetings; Bonhoeffer's *Life Together*, 1939; Peterson's *Working the Angles*, 1987; James K. A. Smith's *Desiring the Kingdom*, 2009) names the same pattern at different historical moments. The corpus's session liturgy stands in this lineage.

**Proposed Law (Structural-Operational): The work the rest of Vol 2 names — the four-connects, the container, the clearings, the hearing-with-understanding, the community amplification, the corporate witness, the tool map, the import discipline — does not happen in pieces.
