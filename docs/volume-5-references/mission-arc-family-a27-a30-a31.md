# Mission-Arc Family (A27 + A30 + A31)

## Mission-Arc Family Protocol (V2.Exp5 — Connect with Mission, with cross-refs to V2.Exp7A, V2.Exp9A, V2.Exp1B)
## What this family protocol does
The Mission-Arc family is the BoB practice's sequenced-arc protocol set for mission development: **A27 Who Are You / Identity Discovery Sequence** (the upstream identity-clarification work), **A30 Shadow Mission Protocol** (the surfacing of the disowned alternative trajectory the participant is tempted to live), and **A31 Mission Stretch Clarity Protocol** (the refinement of articulated mission against operational realities, plus the corporate-witnessed accountability move). The three protocols are operationally distinct but developmentally unified: A27's outputs flow into mission articulation that A30 stress-tests against shadow alternatives and that A31 refines into walked-out specificity with covenant-witnessed accountability. The arc is normally a multi-month progression for a new participant in a BoB or FotH group; mature participants iterate the arc across years as identity, mission, shadow-awareness, and accountability all develop.

The family entry handles three things together: the **sequencing and loop-back structure** (which is itself core), the **shared Christian-frame anthropology** that all three protocols depend on (identity-as-given, mission-as-given, accountability-as-corporate, the arc as Spirit-led rather than Companion-led), and the **cross-protocol Companion discernment** that determines downstream routing when one protocol surfaces material another protocol must engage. Each of the three protocols also has its own operational sub-section below.

**When to use the family arc (versus an individual protocol)**

• **Start the arc with A27 for new participants** who have done sufficient story-telling (A26) to have surfaced the autobiographical material the identity-discovery sequence will work with. The arc's normal entry-point is A27; running A30 or A31 without A27 upstream produces partial work that lacks the identity-anchor the rest of the arc depends on.

• **Run individual protocols when the participant is mid-arc and a specific protocol is the next-move** — e.g., the participant has done A27, articulated a mission, and needs A31's clarity work without yet being ready for A30's shadow surfacing; or the participant has been walking out a mission for some time and needs A30 to surface the shadow-alternative that has emerged as identity-and-mission have matured.

• **Loop back into A27 when a participant's articulated mission no longer fits their developing identity** — common in groups where the participant has grown substantially across years; A27 re-articulation refreshes the upstream anchor.

• **Loop back into A30 when A31's clarity work surfaces persistent gaps** — the gap between articulated mission and lived energy often points to an unaddressed shadow mission; A30 is the next-move tool.

• **Loop back into A31 when life-circumstance changes require mission refinement** — new vocational stage, family transition, geographic change; the articulated mission stays core but its Vision/Action specifics need refresh.

• **Level II Companion competency for the full arc.** A27's trio facilitation and pattern-recognition-as-collaborative-hearing requires Level II; A30's wound/sin/voice triple-discernment is among the highest-care non-Carpet-Style A-list protocols and requires Level II with substantial reps; A31's covenant-witness reframing requires Level II to hold cleanly. Level I Companions can run individual protocols (especially A27 in its straightforward five-question form and A31 in its question-sequence form) under Level II supervision; the full arc with shadow-mission work is Level II territory.

**When NOT to use the arc or individual protocols (failure modes and contraindications)**

**Family-level failure modes.**

• **Running A30 before A27 has been done** is the most common arc-level failure mode. The shadow mission is meaningful only as the inverse of the articulated mission; without A27 having surfaced identity-and-mission material, A30 has nothing to be the shadow *of*, and the surfacing produces free-floating self-criticism rather than meaningful shadow-naming.

• **Treating the arc as one-time linear sequence** rather than iterative practice. A man does not "complete" the Mission-Arc and move on; the arc is the developmental shape of mission-work over years, with each protocol returning as the participant grows. Companions who treat A27 as a one-time orientation activity miss the deepening that re-articulation across years produces.

• **Skipping A31 Stretch step's accountability move because the Companion treats it as redundant with group-meeting attendance.** The Stretch step's *Who will you report to?* names a specific covenant-witness relationship; group-meeting attendance alone does not produce that covenant. The Stretch step is non-negotiable for A31 to complete its work.

• **Compressing the arc into a single session or weekend.** The 2008 ASC Manual treats the arc as a multi-session developmental progression; weekend-workshop compression produces affective intensity without integration. Iterative pacing across weeks-to-months is core.

**A27-specific failure modes.**

• **Treating the five-question sequence as therapist-interpretive analysis.** The pattern-recognition step at the end (hearing the connections and disconnections among the five answers) is collaborative-hearing for what the Spirit is highlighting, not therapist-or-self interpretive analysis. The Companion's discipline is to facilitate group hearing, not to interpret the participant's material.

• **Pressing into the optional fifth question (*****Where do you put your energy?*****) when the participant is depleted or not ready.** The v5 refresh made it optional explicitly. When the participant has run out of energy or when the energy-disjunction (stated mission vs. actual energy) seems likely to produce shame, defer the fifth question to a subsequent session.

• **Using the trio format mechanics without the spiritual-direction frame.** The trio (responder / questioner / recorder) is structurally close to Ignatian *director / directee / silent witness*; without the frame, the trio drifts into coaching-pair-plus-secretary which loses the discernment register.

**A30-specific failure modes.**

• **Slipping into Jungian archetypal-integration framing during the work.** The shadow mission is *disowned alternative trajectory brought into the light*, not Jungian sub-self requiring integration. The Companion must hold the reframing explicitly throughout the work; the failure mode is real and produces a protocol that does adjacent-to-Christian work ending in a place the corpus does not endorse.

• **Failing to discern wound-rooted vs. sin-rooted vs. mixed-rooted shadow material before downstream routing.** A30 surfaces the shadow mission; what to *do* with what is surfaced depends on the root. Wound-rooted routes to A17 family; sin-rooted routes to V2.Exp4 / A6; mixed-rooted requires Spirit-discernment for which pole to address first. The Companion who treats all shadow material identically produces poorly-routed downstream work.

• **Failing to discern the voice the shadow speaks in** (accusation vs. internalized lie vs. Spirit-conviction per V2.Exp7A). Each kind of voice gets a different response: accusation resisted, lie addressed therapeutically, Spirit-conviction heeded and confessed. Voice-discernment is load-bearing before the surfacing matures into work.

• **Surfacing shadow material the participant is not yet ready to receive.** Premature shadow-mission surfacing produces shame or defensive collapse rather than naming-and-resistance. The Companion's discernment of participant-readiness is part of the protocol; deferral to a later cycle is sometimes the right move.

**A31-specific failure modes.**

• **Letting the Stretch step drift into productivity-coaching accountability.** The reporting partner is a covenant-witness fellow-pilgrim, not a task-master. If the Companion lets the framing drift, the protocol does productivity-coaching work in Christian dress.

• **Stopping the clarity loop before sufficient specificity.** Vague mission articulation is a sign that Vision is still general or that Action has not yet been heard. The clarity questions (*What does X mean to you? / What would that look like? / How will you know?*) loop until the articulated mission is concrete enough to be walked out. Companions who close the loop early produce mission statements that cannot be walked.

• **Pressing the Stretch step's *****Who will you report to?***** when no appropriate partner is available.** Sometimes the right initial reporting partner is the Companion themselves until the group's trust matures; sometimes no partner is the right answer for the immediate cycle and the protocol completes without that step (with the Companion noting that A31 has been partially run and will need its Stretch closing at a subsequent cycle). Forcing a partner-selection that does not fit the participant's current relational landscape produces accountability-in-name-only.

**The arc structure and sequencing**

The core arc is **A27 → mission articulation (V2.Exp5 territory) → A30 paired with A31 → iterative loop-backs as needed**.

**Phase 1 — Identity Discovery (A27).** The participant runs the five-question sequence over one or more sessions in trio format. Outputs: articulated working answers to *Who are you / Where are you going / Who are you going with / What do you serve / (optionally) Where do you put your energy*, plus the pattern-recognition step's collaborative hearing of the connections and disconnections. Time: typically one to three group sessions, sometimes spread across weeks.

**Phase 2 — Mission articulation.** The participant moves from A27's identity outputs into a working mission statement, using the Vision + Action = Mission formula. This phase is V2.Exp5 core territory (not a discrete Part VI protocol); A27's outputs flow into V2.Exp5's mission-statement work. The participant emerges from this phase with an articulated working mission.

**Phase 3 — Shadow Mission surfacing (A30).** Once the articulated mission is sufficient, A30 surfaces the disowned alternative — the counterfeit trajectory the participant is tempted to live instead. Outputs: named shadow mission, identified root (wound-rooted / sin-rooted / mixed), identified voice (accusation / lie / Spirit-conviction), downstream routing decision. Time: typically one or two sessions; sometimes the surfacing produces a downstream routing to wound-work or sin-channel work that takes weeks-to-months before the participant returns to the arc.

**Phase 4 — Mission Stretch Clarity (A31).** With identity anchored and shadow named, the participant runs A31 to refine the articulated mission into walked-out specificity and to enter into corporate-witnessed accountability. Outputs: refined Vision and Action, articulated Mission, Stretch commitments (actions, support, reporting partner), entered covenant-witness relationship. Time: one to two sessions for the initial A31; iterative loop-backs across the participant's group life.

**Iterative loop-backs.** Standard across the family. A30 may route to A17 family wound-work or V2.Exp4 / A6 sin-channel work, with eventual return to the arc. A31 may route back to A27 for re-articulation when life-stage changes require fresh identity work, or back to A30 when persistent stretch-execution gaps point to unaddressed shadow material. The arc is iterative-developmental, not linear-completable.

**The protocol — A27 Who Are You / Identity Discovery Sequence**

The five-question sequence run in trio format. Per the Feb 2026 v5 refresh, the language is gender-neutral and the fifth question is explicitly optional.

1. **Trio formation and framing.**

- Companion: form trios (responder / questioner / recorder), or facilitate solo if the group is too small. Frame the work: *"This is a discovery sequence, not a self-assessment. We're listening together for what God has already made true about you and is showing us as we ask."*

- Participants: take roles. The questioner asks; the responder responds; the recorder notes the responses verbatim (not interpretation).

- Discernment: ensure the responder is ready to be the receiver of the sequence; if the participant is in crisis or substantially depleted, defer.

2. **Question 1 — Who are you?**

- Questioner: ask the question simply. *"Who are you?"* Allow silence. Do not rephrase.

- Responder: give whatever surfaces. Multiple answers are normal — the responder may say *"I am a husband. I am a father. I am a son of the Lord. I am someone who is afraid of being forgotten."* Let all of it land.

- Recorder: write what is said.

- Discernment: notice without naming — what does the responder lead with? What surfaces late? What is missing that the Companion would have expected? Hold the noticing.

3. **Question 2 — Where are you going?**

- Questioner: ask. *"Where are you going?"*

- Responder: respond. *Going* may be vocational, relational, geographic, spiritual; let the responder define the register.

- Discernment: where does the responder's *going* connect to their *identity* from Question 1? Where is there a disconnect?

4. **Question 3 — Who are you going with?**

- Questioner: ask.

- Responder: respond. The named companions may be spouse, family, community, the Lord, friends, colleagues; let the responder define.

- Discernment: who is named? Who is absent that you would expect? Is the Lord named, and if so, in what register?

5. **Question 4 — What do you serve?**

- Questioner: ask.

- Responder: respond. *Serving* may name a population (the poor, men in recovery, my children), a value (truth, beauty, justice), a relationship (Christ, the church), or a domain (the work of healing). Let the responder define.

- Discernment: does the response have specificity sufficient to be a service-direction, or does it stay at the value-statement level?

6. **Question 5 (optional) — Where do you put your energy?**

- Companion: discern whether to press. The v5 refresh names this question optional; if the responder has been depleted, defer.

- Questioner: if proceeding, ask.

- Responder: respond, but answer descriptively rather than aspirationally — *not where you wish your energy went, but where it actually goes*.

- Discernment: the gap between Questions 1–4 (stated identity-mission-companions-service) and Question 5 (actual energy) is the diagnostic load-bearing material. It often points at the shadow mission A30 later surfaces.

7. **Pattern recognition (collaborative hearing).**

- Companion: invite the group (or trio) into listening. *"What connections do we hear among these five? Where do we hear disconnections? What is the Spirit highlighting?"*

- Group/trio: speak briefly what is heard — not interpretive analysis, but what landed as significant.

- Responder: receive what is offered; may name what they themselves hear in the pattern.

- Discernment: hold the receiving in V2.Exp7A discernment-of-voices register; what is offered is Spirit-led hearing, not therapist-interpretive analysis.

8. **Closing.**

- Companion: close the session per the standard V2.Exp8 marked-closing form. The articulated material from this session is now part of the participant's working identity-and-mission record; subsequent sessions may refine, deepen, or revisit.

**The protocol — A30 Shadow Mission Protocol**

The surfacing-naming-resisting protocol for the disowned alternative trajectory. Held in the **reframed Christian-frame anthropology**: the shadow mission is *disowned alternative the participant is tempted to live*, not Jungian archetypal sub-mission requiring integration.

1. **Framing and reframing.**

- Companion: name the work explicitly. *"We're going to look at the shadow mission — the alternative life you are tempted to live instead of the one God has been showing you. The word 'shadow' is borrowed from Jungian work; we use it differently. We're not integrating a hidden sub-self. We're bringing into the light what you have been tempted to live as a counterfeit of the real mission, so it can be named and resisted under the Lord's authority. The pattern is Matthew 4 — Jesus in the wilderness named the three alternatives and refused them."*

- Participant: receive the framing.

- Discernment: confirm the participant has an articulated working mission to be the shadow *of*. Without that anchor, defer to A27 / mission articulation work first.

2. **Surface the shadow.**

- Companion: ask. *"What is the alternative life you are tempted to live? What is the version of yourself that would have made the choices that look successful but were not actually what God was asking? Let it be specific."*

- Participant: surface what is there. Specificity matters — *"I am tempted to be the man who makes money to prove he is worth something"* is operationally useful; *"I am tempted to be selfish"* is too vague to work with.

- Discernment: the surfacing may take time. Hold silence. Notice what the participant resists naming; the resistance often marks the shadow's location.

3. **Name the root.**

- Companion: ask. *"What is this shadow rooted in? Is it a wound that wants compensation? Is it material you have not yet confessed? Is it both?"*

- Participant: respond from what they perceive.

- Discernment (V2.Exp1B governs): the Companion holds the wound/sin distinction. Wound-rooted shadow points toward downstream A17 family work. Sin-rooted shadow points toward V2.Exp4 / A6 work. Mixed-rooted (the common case) requires Spirit-discernment for which pole to address first. Where the participant cannot yet discern the root, the Companion may name what is observable: *"I notice this looks like both. Let's hold both."*

4. **Name the voice.**

- Companion: ask. *"What voice is the shadow speaking in? Is it accusing you (you'll never be the real thing; settle for the counterfeit)? Is it speaking as if it is who you actually are (the counterfeit is who I really am)? Is it the Spirit convicting you (the counterfeit really is what you have been living)?"*

- Participant: discern.

- Discernment (V2.Exp7A governs): the Companion holds the three-fold voice distinction. Accusation → resist, do not negotiate, stand in V2.Exp9A identity. Lie → address therapeutically through the relevant wound-or-cognitive-blockage protocol. Spirit-conviction → heed, confess, repent. The voice's kind determines the next move.

5. **Resist under the Lord's authority.**

- Companion: frame the resisting move. *"Whatever voice we have identified, the move is the same in form**. We** name what we have heard, we name it as not who God is calling you to be, and we ask the Lord to make the true mission solid enough that the counterfeit loses its pull. This is not integration. This is bringing what was in the darkness into the light, so it can lose its power."*

- Participant: speak the naming and the resisting aloud. *"I have been tempted to [shadow mission]. I name it as the counterfeit. Lord, make the real mission solid in me."* The exact words vary; the structure (name → name as counterfeit → ask the Lord) is core.

- Discernment: receive the speaking; do not press for affective intensity; the work is the speaking and the receiving, not the feeling-tone.

6. **Downstream routing decision.**

- Companion: name the next-move. *"Based on what we discerned about the root and the voice, the next-move work is [A17 family for wound-rooted / V2.Exp4 confession for sin-rooted / a return to A27 for mixed-rooted where the identity-anchor needs refreshing / a return to A31 for stretch-execution work if the shadow has been named clearly enough]."* The downstream work is its own protocol at its own session.

- Participant: receive the routing.

7. **Closing.**

- Companion: close per the standard V2.Exp8 marked-closing form. If a V2.Exp9A corporate-witness affirmation is appropriate (the group has heard what God has done in this surfacing), it may close the session; in the more common early-cycle case the closing is simpler and the affirmation is reserved for downstream work that produces the clearer identity-claim.

**The protocol — A31 Mission Stretch Clarity Protocol**

The clarity-and-accountability protocol that refines articulated mission and enters the covenant-witnessed accountability.

1. **State your mission.**

- Companion: invite. *"State your mission as you currently understand it."*

- Participant: state. The articulated mission from V2.Exp5 work (informed by A27 outputs and shaped by A30 shadow-awareness) is the working text.

2. **Vision clarity loop.**

- Companion: ask the clarity questions. *"What do you want? What does that mean to you? What would that look like? How will you know?"*

- Participant: respond, allowing each question to refine the prior answer.

- Discernment: continue the loop until the Vision is specific enough that the participant could describe what walking in it looks like in concrete terms. Vague Vision after multiple loops is a sign that the hearing-work is incomplete; the Companion may route the participant back into V2.Exp7 hearing practices before continuing.

3. **Action clarity loop.**

- Companion: ask. *"What are you doing to get it? What does that look like in your week? How will you know?"*

- Participant: respond, allowing the loop to refine.

- Discernment: the Action must be concrete and measurable. *"I will pray more"* is not yet Action; *"I will spend twenty minutes in PROAPT three mornings a week with a paper journal"* is. The clarity loop produces this specificity.

4. **Vision + Action = Mission.**

- Companion: name the integration. *"The Vision you have named and the Action you have committed to — together they are your mission as you can articulate it now. Let's say it together."*

- Participant: state the integrated mission.

5. **How is that working?**

- Companion: ask. *"How is it working? Are you getting what you want? How do you know?"*

- Participant: respond honestly. Where the Action is not producing the Vision, the gap is data — sometimes the Action needs refinement, sometimes the Vision was not yet heard correctly, sometimes a shadow mission has been quietly diverting energy.

- Discernment: significant gaps may route the participant back to A30 (shadow material may be diverting) or back to V2.Exp7 hearing practices (Vision may need re-hearing) or back to A27 (identity may have shifted under the participant such that the mission no longer fits).

6. **Stretch step.**

- Companion: name the three Stretch questions. *"Stretch — what specific actions are you committing to in the next defined period? Do you want support? Who will you report to?"*

- Participant: respond to each. The specific actions are the operational obediences; the support question opens the door to group help; the reporting partner is the covenant-witness for the period.

- Discernment (the reframing matters): the reporting partner is a fellow-pilgrim covenant-witness, not a productivity-coaching task-master. The Companion frames the Stretch step in the 1 Timothy 6:12 / Hebrews 10:24–25 register. Where no clear reporting partner is available, the Companion may serve until a group-member partner emerges, or the protocol may close partial with the reporting-partner question to be revisited next cycle.

7. **Corporate witness (V2.Exp9A).**

- Companion: invite the group's witness. *"You have heard [Participant]'s mission and Stretch commitments. Is there anything you want to speak back to them as witness?"* This is brief — the group's role is to affirm what the Lord has shown, not to add commentary.

- Group: speak briefly if there is something to speak. Silence is also an appropriate witness.

8. **Closing.**

- Companion: close per the standard V2.Exp8 marked-closing form, with the affirmation pattern per V2.Exp9A applied if appropriate.

**Closing and de-roling (if applicable)**

**Not applicable to the family's own protocols.** None of A27, A30, or A31 involves role-play in the carpet-work sense. The de-roling protocol per A14 / refined V2.Exp8 is not invoked at the close of any Mission-Arc session. The container closing uses the standard V2.Exp8 marked-closing form.

**One adjacent consideration.** A30's downstream routing may move into A17 family work (carpet-style, role-play involved); the de-roling per A14 applies to *that* subsequent work, not to A30 itself. Companions running A30 should not import role-play prematurely into the shadow-mission protocol — turning the shadow-naming into an empty-chair conversation with the counterfeit-self mid-protocol is a category error.

## Companion notes
The arc is one practice in three movements, and the Companion's primary discipline is to hold the arc-as-arc rather than treating the three protocols as discrete tools that happen to sit near each other. A man who has done A27 once, A30 once, and A31 once has done initial work on the arc; a man who has done the arc many times across years, with substantial loop-backs and downstream-routing engagements, has done the deeper work the practice is for. Hold the long view.

The Jungian-reframing for A30 is the single most consequential piece of the family-treatment, and it requires the Companion to be solid in the reframing before facilitating. If the Companion drifts into archetypal-integration framing during the work, the protocol does adjacent-to-Christian work that ends in a place the corpus does not endorse. Read the Carpet-Style family Phase 2's A23 reframing alongside this entry; the same reframing applies, with the same scriptural anchors and the same operational discipline. If you have not yet facilitated A23 or the Carpet-Style family's reframing in carpet-work, observe several A30 facilitations by a senior Companion before leading.

The wound/sin/voice triple-discernment at A30's heart is the most discernment-dense moment in the arc. Hold the three distinctions consciously. Wound-rooted shadow → A17 family downstream. Sin-rooted shadow → V2.Exp4 / A6 downstream. Mixed-rooted (the common case) → Spirit-discernment for first pole. Accusation voice → resist, V2.Exp9A standing-identity. Lie voice → therapeutic engagement. Spirit-conviction voice → heed and confess. The Companion who routes downstream work poorly here produces months of misaligned downstream effort.

The Stretch step's reframing in A31 is the second most consequential reframing in the family. The reporting partner is a fellow-pilgrim covenant-witness, not a task-master who enforces compliance. Frame the partner-selection accordingly: *who walks closely enough with you and with the Lord to discern alongside you*, not *who will hold you most accountable*. The wrong partner-selection produces accountability-in-name-only or productivity-coaching in Christian dress; the right partner-selection produces years of mission-walking with witness.

A small operational note. The arc deliberately leaves the meta-pacing as Companion-discernment. How often A27 cycles re-run, how soon after mission articulation A30 is facilitated, how often A31 Stretch commitments are revisited — these vary by participant and by group developmental phase. Mature groups develop their own rhythm. Early-group default: A27 within the first three months of participation; A30 within the first year; A31 quarterly thereafter with full re-runs annually. Deviate intentionally based on what the participant needs.

A final note on the §5.1(c) gender-framing posture. The Feb 2026 v5 *Who Are You* refresh has already done the gender-neutral language adaptation for A27. A30 and A31 in the 2008 manual carry men's-group idiom (*shadow* in particular has masculine-Warrior-Work resonance even in its IJH reframing); the FotH practice is in active development on how the shadow-mission and stretch language land in women's-group and mixed-group settings. The §5.1(c) pastoral-pattern + ongoing-workshopping posture applies. The BoB form is core; the FotH form is developing alongside; specific question-wordings may adapt as FotH practice matures.
