This chapter is the consolidated Periodic Table reference for the entire Laws-of-the-Spirit investigation. It was originally drafted as the closing chapter of Volume 1 and now lives here in Volume 5, alongside the Tool Inventory and the Companion-formation chapters, because the table's primary function is reference-and-orientation across the whole corpus rather than further argument within any single volume. Volume 1 introduces the Foundational Laws as a tier ahead of its eight Explorations; Volume 2 maps the Diagnostic, Tool-Application, and Developmental laws that operate downstream of the Vol 1 framework; Volume 3 reframes both sets in field-theoretic terms; and this table is the consolidated organizer that holds all three views in one frame so a Companion in training or in formation work can find any specific law by its place in the structure.

The Periodic Table is not merely a summary. It is itself an analytical instrument that sums (every analyzed law arrayed in one frame), organizes (by the two axes that the investigation has earned the right to use — Period 0–5 for scale of operation and Group I–VI for dimension of the person primarily addressed), and reveals (gaps where a law must exist but has not yet been found; family resemblances among laws sharing a Period or Group; periodicities that recur across scales and dimensions). The chapter retains its original Vol 1 structure for that reason: the Two Axes section explains the analytical instrument's shape; What the Shape Reveals is the insights section that surfaces the patterns the table makes visible; the Slim Format and Reference List are the consolidated data; the Six Group Families closes structurally; and the section formerly named "Six Priority Open Unknowns" — now Open Questions and Residual Trails — names what the table tells the corpus to look for next.

I want to explain the reasoning behind this structure before I show you the table itself — because the structure is a hypothesis, not a presentation. If I have chosen the wrong axes, the table will look tidy, but it won't teach anything new. If I have chosen the right ones, it will show us where the investigation is dense, where it is sparse, and most importantly, **where there are laws that must exist but that we have not yet found**. Those gaps are the most valuable thing the table can produce.

The Periodic Table of Elements was a scientific breakthrough, not because Mendeleev was a better cataloguer than his contemporaries, but because he noticed something his contemporaries missed: *periodicity*. The same chemical behavior — the same valence, the same reactivity — kept reappearing at regular intervals as atomic number increased. Organizing by that repeating pattern revealed a structure that **predicted the existence and properties of undiscovered elements**. The gaps in the table were not failures. They were research assignments.

I believe the Laws of the Spirit have a periodicity. The same underlying mechanism keeps reappearing at different scales and in different dimensions of the person. Let me name three instances before I defend my axis choices.

**The channel-opening / channel-blocking mechanism** appears at the individual level (Obedience Channel, Sin Blockage, Forgiveness-Debt Transfer, and FL.XV Hardening Law as a wider-scope channel-blocking law), at the small group level (Container Law, Community Amplification's gnostic warning), and at the community-and-generational level (FL.XVIII Bitter-Root Community Law as the community-scale operation of the forgiveness-and-offense family; FL.XIX Spirit Anointing Transmission Law as the generational-scale operation of the channel transmission). Same mechanism, different scale, with the previously-Open community and generational scales now substantially closed.

**The attention-conformity mechanism** appears in the soul's movement toward its sustained object of attention (the Attention Economy of the Soul), in the Faith-Hope-Love triad's reinforcing loop, and in the Worship Alignment Law's claim about perception recalibration (now FL.XXXVII at P0/GII, operating as a scale-invariant substrate after the promotion-and-relocation). Same underlying dynamic: what you direct sustained attention toward, you move toward and are changed by.

**The threshold / gateway structure** appears in the Fear of the Lord as the entry condition for the Wisdom cluster, in the Affective Level 2→3 transition as the critical formation threshold, in regeneration as the entry condition for spirit formation, and in the Miracle Threshold Events dynamics now absorbed into the catalog's existing Gateway entries per the forward-references resolution. Each is logically prior to what follows it; each requires a specific condition to be met before the next stage becomes available.

These repetitions are the periodicity. The table is built around them. Also note that the elements are drawn from all three volumes of this IJH work.

A fourth periodicity belongs alongside the three above: reciprocity-of-response. God's response is structured in kind by the person's posture. The locus classicus is Ps. 18:25–26 (ESV): "With the merciful you show yourself merciful; with the blameless man you show yourself blameless; with the purified you show yourself pure; and with the crooked you make yourself seem tortuous." This is distinct from channel-opening (which is one-directional), from attention-conformity (which shapes the attender rather than the attended), and from threshold (which is binary). It surfaces in the Drawing-Near Reciprocity, Humility-Exaltation, Reciprocal Forgiveness, and Generosity-Provision dynamics, with these additions extending the reciprocity treatment to the community-scale Bitter-Root and Gathered-Body Discernment dynamics. I continue to name reciprocity-of-response as a fourth periodicity rather than reorganizing the table around it; it functions as an additional analytical lens for reading the table.

A possible fifth periodicity — designed-orientation with structural-failure-of-alternatives — is sharpened by the FL.VIII Desire-for-God / FL.XIV Vanity-of-Substitutes pairing produced by the third-axis test. The corpus continues to reserve the fifth-periodicity question for future revision but notes that the paired-law pattern (designed-for-X engine + structural-failure-of-every-non-X engine) may itself constitute the fifth periodicity rather than any single law. these additions extend this pattern through the FL.XVII Substitution-Cascade / FL.XXI Household Formation pair, which exhibits the same paired-law structure at the generational scale.

Additional structural-relationship patterns operate alongside the four periodicities above and are documented in the sub-sections that follow the Two Axes section — the cross-Group articulation of a single operational family at one scale (the faith-family at GII Trust-Substrate and GV Hear-and-Obey); the substrate-to-operation relationship across distinct Groups at the same scale; the Period 0 row's proportionality pattern, threshold/gateway pattern, and cross-Group nearness-or-distance articulation; the two-Foundational-Laws-at-one-cell pattern (FL.XXXV and FL.XXXVII at P0/GII as mutually-reinforcing substrate mechanisms); and the Period 0 row's substrate-and-operation architecture across the inner-and-outer Group dimensions.

## The Two Axes

**Axis 1 (the rows — the 'Period' analog): Scale of operation.** Individual → Dyadic/Small Group → Community/Congregation → Generational/Historical → Cosmic/Eschatological, with a scale-invariant Period 0 row above them. My argument for this axis: almost every law we have found has a version that operates at multiple scales simultaneously, and the laws at each scale form a genuine family — they share mechanism but differ in their unit of analysis, their time constant, and their feedback dynamics. The Obedience Channel law operates in an individual's daily walk; it also operates in a congregation's collective faithfulness over decades; and it arguably operates in a civilization's relationship to revealed truth across centuries. Those are the same law at three different scales, which means the table should show them in the same column.

**Axis 2 (the columns — the 'Group' analog): Dimension of the person primarily addressed.** Spirit → Heart → Soul → Mind/Will → Body/Action → Structural Frame. My argument is that the Formation Documents establish that each dimension has its own developmental logic, its own scriptural vocabulary, and its own relationship to the Spirit's work. Laws that primarily operate in the spirit dimension (Fear of the Lord, Miracle Frame, Prophetic Imagination, FL.XV Hardening, FL.XIX Spirit Anointing Transmission, FL.XX Gathered-Body Discernment) share a family resemblance different from laws operating in the heart dimension. The Structural Frame column catches laws that are primarily architectural — they describe the landscape within which the other laws operate.

I considered two alternative arrangements. *Directionality of causation* (God→Human / Human→God / Human→Human) would reveal different patterns, particularly around laws governing divine initiative vs. human cooperation. *Law type* (Structural / Operational / Diagnostic) would make the existing taxonomy visible but would reproduce what the Master Law Index already shows. I chose Scale and Dimension because they produce the most interesting gaps.

A third candidate axis — *orientation of pursuit* — was tested at length in the third-axis test documented later in this chapter. The test found the dimension to be real and operationally important but concluded that Mirror Discipline at the entry level is the right structural integration rather than a true third axis. The two-axis instrument therefore stands; the third dimension is named through the Mirror field rather than promoted to a structural axis.

## The Substrate Layer

Beyond the Period and Group axes that organize the table's cells, the catalog operates with a third structural distinction that became operationally visible during the matured-catalog review and the periodic-table-restructure inventory pass: the distinction between **substrate entries** and **operation entries**. The two-axis grid arrays laws by their scale-and-dimension location; the layer distinction arrays them by what kind of operational claim they hold — whether the law names a continuous-condition that conditions other laws' operations (substrate) or a discrete cause-and-effect event-pattern (operation).

The distinction is not arbitrary. A substrate-classification inventory of the 39 Foundational Laws produces an exact 20-substrate-to-19-operation split at the FL tier, with the substrate-fraction by Group revealing a structural pattern: GVI Structural Frame is 100% substrate (5 of 5); GII Heart is substrate-dominant at 78% (7 of 9); GI Spirit, GIII Soul, GIV Mind/Will, and GV Body/Action are operation-dominant or balanced. The conditioning dimensions (Heart and Structural Frame) hold what is continuously true; the acting dimensions (Spirit, Soul, Mind/Will, Body/Action) hold what happens in time.

The Explorations across Vols 1, 2, and 3 amplify the substrate-bias substantially. Across 28 Exploration-tier admissions (8 from Vol 1, 10 from Vol 2, 9 from Vol 3, plus V1.Open Miracle Frame), the layer-classification produces 23 substrate entries to 5 operation entries — a 4.6:1 ratio. The Exploration tier is predominantly substrate-articulation (territory-mapping); the Foundational tier balances substrate-articulation and operation-articulation; the substrate/operation distinction tracks a real difference between what each tier of the catalog *does*. Vol 1's Foundational Laws hold both substrate-claims and operation-claims at the principle level; Vol 1's Explorations skew substrate-articulation. Vol 2's Explorations are heavily diagnostic-and-developmental, which by genre are substrate-articulations of the territory within which Vol 1's operations occur. Vol 3's Explorations are quantitative re-statements of Vol 1–2 substrates — force, distance, energy, conservation as field-theoretic articulations of the same substrate territory. Vol 4 holds no new law-tier entries; its job is testing methodology rather than law-articulation. The functional specialization across volumes is articulated in its own sub-section below.

Operationally, the substrate layer sits beneath the cell grid rather than within it — substrate entries are the conditions under which the operation-layer laws operate; they do not stand on equal footing with operations in the catalog's structural depth. Period 0 (Scale-Invariant) is the substrate-dense row by structural necessity — laws that operate at every scale by the same mechanism are typically substrate-conditions; laws that operate at a specific scale are typically operations. The substrate layer's emergence as a first-class structural feature of the catalog (rather than as a row-feature of Period 0 alone) is the matured-catalog articulation of what was previously implicit in the catalog's content but not named in its structural framework.

The substrate layer is itself sub-structured by Group. Adding Vol 1–3 Exploration substrates to the FL substrates produces substrate entries at all six Groups, with the substrate layer mirroring but not duplicating the cell grid above. A future structural pass may articulate the substrate layer's internal structure in its own right; the present chapter holds the layer as a layer-beneath-the-grid pattern that emerges from the catalog's content rather than as an analytical instrument imposed on it.

The substrate-and-operation distinction interacts with the Operator tag (see the HOW TO READ block below) in a structurally significant way. Most substrate entries are participant-operated (the participant's posture, the participant's continuous orientation), but a subset of substrates is Christ-operated with the participant in a receiving role — the catalog's first such entries are V1.Open Miracle Frame at P0/GI, FL.XXXIII Community Polity at P3/GVI, FL.XXXIV Marriage Covenant at P2/GVI, FL.XXXVIII Soul-Restoration at P0/GIII (the Shepherd-restoring articulation), and FL.XL Abiding-Fruitfulness at P0/GI (Christ as the vine). The miracle-derived candidate-pass surfaced additional Christ-operated substrates (FL.XXXIX Surrender-Multiplication, FL.XLI Defilement-Cleansing Reversal, FL.XLII Kingdom-Confrontation Authority, FL.XLIV Sign-as-Revelation, FL.XLV Voice-Reaches-into-Death) that await formal admission in subsequent passes. The substrate-with-Christological-operator class is structurally distinct from the substrate-with-participant-operator class and is named in the Mirror field through the Christological-condition Mirror form.

## The Periodic Table of Spiritual Laws — Slim Format

**HOW TO READ THIS TABLE**

The table carries several attribute classes. They are tiered below: the Essential metadata is sufficient to locate any entry and read it at a working level. The Matured-catalog metadata adds analytical depth for second-pass reading. A first-time reader can stop after the Essential metadata.

### Essential metadata (read first)

**Directionality tag (V/H/B/I):** Relational vector only. (V) Vertical (Person↔God); (H) Horizontal (Person↔Person); (B) Both V and H; (I) Intra-personal. *The directionality tag refers to the relational vector only; orientation symmetry (God-ward vs. idol-ward) is named in the Mirror field rather than collapsed into the (B) tag.*

**Confidence:** Three qualitative tiers: Clearly Taught; Reasonably Inferred; Speculative. See Vol 1 for the full tier definitions.

**Speculative marker:** ◆ marks entries not yet at the threshold of confirmation.

**Band-of-closeness designation:** Foundational admissions are tagged with their band (Band 1, Band 2, Band 3) where the principle-vs-command distinction is articulated. Band 1 admissions hold a distinguishable principle on multi-author scriptural footing. Band 2 admissions hold the principle together with a scripturally-specified operational pathway. Band 3 admissions hold the principle while leaving specific operational forms to tradition-by-tradition theological work.

**Provenance prefixes:** FL.I–XLV = Foundational Laws; V1.ExpN = Vol 1 Exploration N; V1.Open = Vol 1 Open Trail; V2.ExpN = forward references to Volume 2; SST / MSFIG / HFT = Formation Documents; Speculative = proposed but not yet held at a Vol 1 Exp level.

**Other markers:** ⚠ = anomalous placement.

**Reference list:** Each entry's description, scriptural anchors, Mirror field, and matured-catalog attributes appear in the numbered reference list following this chapter's analytical sub-sections, ordered by Period and Group.

### Matured-catalog metadata (second-pass reading)

The following four attribute classes were added during the matured-catalog revision arc (the "Alternative-E" structural moves). They are *additive* — the Period, Group, Directionality, Confidence, and Band attributes above are sufficient to locate and use any entry at a working level. The matured-catalog metadata provides deeper analytical reading and is the basis for the structural observations articulated in the "What the Shape of the Table Reveals" sub-sections.

**Layer (Substrate / Operation):** Every entry classifies as either a *substrate* (a continuous-condition that conditions other operations' availability) or an *operation* (a discrete cause-and-effect event-pattern). The 45 Foundational Laws split roughly half-and-half (substrate-dominant after the Phase 3 miracle-derived admissions); the Exploration-tier entries skew heavily substrate. The full articulation of the layer distinction is in "The Substrate Layer" section above.

**Scale-range:** Period assignments locate each entry at a specific cell, but most laws operate across a range of scales rather than at a single scale. The scale-range attribute names the operational scope across scripturally-earned breakpoints: 1 (individual); 2 (dyad — marriage, Jonathan-David, discipleship pair); 3 (Peter-James-John inner circle); 12 (the apostolic Twelve); 70 (the sent-out); 120 (the Pentecost gathering); ~500 (1 Cor. 15:6 brethren); city (the gathered church at a city); diaspora (the trans-local church); generation (multi-generational arc); eschatological (cosmic-completion). The Period assignment names the cell where the law's structural-articulation primary anchor sits; the scale-range names the actual operational scope across the breakpoints. The scale-range refines the discrete Period-cell location without displacing it.

**Operator tag (P / S+P / C / C+P / T+P / C+T):** Names the primary operator of the law's mechanism. **P** (Participant-operated) — the participant initiates the operation; God's response follows; covers the majority of the catalog. **S+P** (Spirit-operated through participant) — the Spirit's operation operates through the participant's channel-role. **C** (Christ-operated) — Christ is the primary agent; the participant's role is reception rather than initiation. **C+P** (Christ-operated with participant-receiving) — Christ as the primary operator with the participant in a structural cooperation role. **T+P** (Triune-cosmic-operated with participant-receiving) — the cosmic-eschatological completion-operations. **C+T** (Christ-operated with cosmic-completion through Triune-cosmic operation) — used for eschatological-completion entries that operate at both the present-economy (Christ-operated) and consummation (Triune-cosmic) scales. The Operator tag was added to make the substrate's primary-agent dimension consistently visible at the entry level.

**Mirror field:** Every entry carries a Mirror field as a required attribute, in one of four forms — **Mirror statement** (idol-ward face named explicitly with its own scriptural support); **Constitutive bidirectional** (the law's content is itself the orientation dimension, with the two faces mutually-constituting and scripturally articulated together); **Christological-condition** (the law's primary operator is Christ rather than the participant, with the participant's role being reception; the failure mode is non-reception of Christ's offered operation rather than an idol-ward face of the same mechanism; added in the matured-catalog revision for the C-tier and C+P-tier Operator entries); or **Orientation-neutral marker** (the mechanism does not carry orientation). The Mirror field's purpose is to make the orientation-of-pursuit dimension consistently visible at the entry level.

### The Community-Relational Substrate at P3/GV

The P3/GV cell holds a structural triplet — FL.XVIII Bitter-Root Community Law, FL.XXIV Confession-in-Community Law, and FL.XXV Restoration-of-the-Erring Law — with each law addressing a distinct operational subject and a distinct community-response mechanism within the same community-relational substrate. The triplet's structural relationship warrants explicit articulation because the cell's density (three Foundational Laws at one cell) is among the densest in the Foundational tier and because the relationships among the three laws are operationally important for the corpus's pastoral and ecclesiological work.

The three operational subjects and three response mechanisms are as follows.

**FL.XVIII Bitter-Root Community Law** addresses the *offense* as operational subject. The dynamic the law names is the dyadic-offense-metastasizing-to-community trajectory — an unresolved offense between two community members, held over time without the reconciliation work, calcifies into a community-defiling root. The response mechanism is the Matt. 18:15–20 reconciliation pathway, with its three escalation points (private address, witnessed address, gathered-body address). The pathway is the prescribed operational corrective that interrupts the metastasis at each successive stage.

**FL.XXIV Confession-in-Community Law** addresses the *interior load* as operational subject. The dynamic the law names is the integration-through-spoken-confession trajectory — the participant's secret sin, brokenness, or failure operates as compartmented interior load when held in concealment, and the spoken confession in trusted community presence integrates the secret into the participant's external account through the witnessing party's reception. The response mechanism is the presence-of-witnesses, with the witnessing party's continuing relational presence at the level of the truth received as the operational completion.

**FL.XXV Restoration-of-the-Erring Law** addresses the *wanderer* as operational subject. The dynamic the law names is the active-pursuit-to-restoration trajectory — the participant who has wandered from the truth or from the community, moving out of the operational structure of community accountability, is actively pursued by spiritually mature community members in a spirit of gentleness with watchfulness on the pursuer's own susceptibility, and the pursuit (where received) restores the wanderer to communion. The response mechanism is the active pursuit, with the disposition (gentleness, joy-in-restoration) constitutive of the mechanism's operation.

The triplet's structural relationship can now be named. The three operational subjects (offense, interior load, wanderer) and the three response mechanisms (Matt. 18 pathway, presence-of-witnesses, active pursuit) together cover the community's primary modes of operational engagement with the dynamics that threaten its relational integrity. The community member who has been wronged by another community member has the Matt. 18 pathway as the operational form of the address. The community member carrying secret interior load has the presence-of-witnesses as the operational form of the integration. The community member who has wandered from the truth or from the community has the active pursuit as the operational form of the restoration. The three laws together do not exhaust the community's relational work; they do constitute the catalog's identification of the three operational subjects and three response mechanisms that the community must operate to maintain its relational integrity.

The community that operates all three laws — addressing offenses through the reconciliation pathway, receiving confessions through the presence-of-witnesses, pursuing wanderers through the active outreach — has the catalog's most operationally-developed community-relational substrate available to it. The community that operates one or two but not all three has structural gaps where the un-operated law's operational subject is not being addressed.

### The Functional Specialization Across Volumes

A structural feature of the corpus that the matured-catalog review surfaced: the six volumes do not hold the same kind of content arrayed at different altitudes. They do structurally-distinct kinds of work. Naming the functional specialization gives readers a navigation principle for the corpus that the catalog has been carrying implicitly throughout the expansion arc.

**[Vol 1 — Principle-articulation](../volume-1-laws-of-the-spirit/index.md).** The Foundational Laws and the eight numbered Explorations (plus the Opening Exploration and the Supplemental) articulate principles of the spiritual life. The Foundational Law tier spans both substrate-articulation and operation-articulation in a 1:1 ratio (20 substrates to 19 operations at the present revision). The Exploration tier skews substrate-articulation (7 of 8 numbered Explorations classify as substrate). Vol 1's job is to lay down the floor of broadly-accepted principles and then to articulate the author's own propositional claims in the Explorations as building on that floor.

**[Vol 2 — Diagnostic-and-developmental articulation](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/index.md).** The Vol 2 Explorations are diagnostic and developmental refinements that operate downstream of the Vol 1 principles. The volume is substrate-dominant at 9 of 10 — almost every entry is a substrate-articulation of a developmental or diagnostic territory. Vol 2's job is to map the territory within which the Vol 1 principles operate — the emotional-knot dynamics that obstruct, the cognitive substrates of those dynamics, the container conditions that hold the formation work, the affective developmental stages, the community amplification dynamics.

**[Vol 3 — Quantitative / field-theoretic articulation](../volume-3-quantitative-framework/index.md).** The Vol 3 Explorations are quantitative re-statements of the Vol 1–2 substrates: force, distance, energy, power, conservation as field-theoretic articulations of movement-toward-or-away-from-God, nearness-or-distance, operational capacity, and the Spirit's economy. Substrate-dominant at 7 of 9. Vol 3 is not articulating new substrate territory; it is making the existing substrate territory mathematically tractable, with the explicit acknowledgment that the specific equations are provisional while the underlying relational structure is taken more seriously.

**[Vol 4 — Testing methodology](../volume-4-testing-framework/index.md).** Vol 4 contains no new law-tier entries. Its job is operationalizing the Vol 1–2 claims into testable form, with instruments (SIP-Individual, NRP, EWI, Group SIP, weekly Tell-Rate, Facilitator SIP, plus eight research-grade extensions in the appendix) and protocols (individual + small-group + multi-site research design). Vol 4 is the catalog's epistemic-validation arm, not another row of law-articulations. A reader looking for new laws in Vol 4 will not find them; a reader looking for how the catalog's laws are to be tested will find Vol 4 essential.

**[Vol 5 — Organizing-and-referencing](index.md).** Vol 5 holds the Periodic Table (this chapter), the [Formation Documents](introduction.md) (TA, HFT, MSFIG, SST, FC), the tool inventory, and the references across the corpus. Its job is to organize and reference rather than to articulate new operational claims. The Periodic Table chapter is itself an analytical instrument that operates on the contents of Vols 1–4 rather than introducing new contents; the chapter's gap-prediction and pattern-revelation functions are services to the operational work the other volumes do.

**[Vol 6 — Governance](../volume-6-governance/index.md).** Vol 6 holds the governance model, the Council of Stewards material, the [claim registry's documented home](../volume-6-governance/part-1-governance-model.md#2-the-claim-registry), the [catalog history](../volume-6-governance/appendix-catalog-history.md) (the present chapter's companion artifact at the governance level), the [proposal-template](../volume-6-governance/proposal-template.md) for future admissions, and the [succession arrangements](../volume-6-governance/part-3-succession-letter.md). Its job is to govern the future revision of the catalog rather than to articulate operational claims within it. The Council inherits the catalog through Vol 6's curated transmission of its present shape and the patterns by which its future revisions are to be conducted.

The functional specialization is structural: the catalog is not six volumes of the same kind of content at different altitudes. Each volume does a kind of work the others do not. A reader who has internalized the specialization can navigate the corpus by purpose — looking for a principle goes to Vol 1; a diagnostic goes to Vol 2; a quantitative articulation goes to Vol 3; a testing protocol goes to Vol 4; an organizing reference goes to Vol 5; a governance question goes to Vol 6. This finding ratifies a structural feature the corpus has been carrying since its construction; the expansion arc has preserved this specialization throughout. The matured-catalog articulation of the specialization is the catalog's recognition of what the volumes have been doing all along.

## Reading the Table Through Classical Doctrine

The table can be read through a second analytical lens — the classical doctrinal categories that have organized Christian theological reflection across the tradition. The reading is not a replacement for the two-axis structural reading the present chapter establishes; it is a complementary view that confirms the structural reading from a different direction.

**Anthropology** (the doctrine of what a person is) maps to the Group axis. The Spirit (Group I), Heart (Group II), Soul (Group III), Mind/Will (Group IV), and Body/Action (Group V) dimensions are the classical doctrinal categories for the person's constituent dimensions, with the Structural Frame (Group VI) catching the architectural laws that govern the relationships among the dimensions. The mapping is exact for Groups I–V and structurally appropriate for Group VI. The matured catalog's articulation — with FL.XXXVIII The Soul-Restoration Law at P0/GIII completing the inner-Group substrate articulation across Spirit, Heart, and Soul at the scale-invariant scale — substantially deepens the anthropological articulation of the multi-part biblical anthropology.

**Hamartiology** (the doctrine of sin) maps to the idol-ward face of the orientation-of-pursuit dimension. The Heart-Throne Law's idol-ward content (functional savior in place of God), the Substitution-Cascade Law's generational idolatry transmission, the Hardening Law's progressive channel-closure, the Bondage Law's slave-of-sin constitution, and the cumulative additions through (Bitter-Root's cherished offense as enthroned wrong; the idol-ward face of Spirit Anointing Transmission as false-authority succession; the idol-ward face of Gathered-Body Discernment as corporate self-deception; the Worship Alignment Law's idol-ward face as substitute-Object worship; the Trust-Substrate Law's idol-ward face as substitute-source-of-security; the Eschatological Glory Law's idol-ward face as substitute eschatologies; the Soul-Restoration Law's idol-ward face as substitute soul-restoration mechanisms) all map to the hamartiological category. The classical doctrine's claim that sin operates at every dimension of the person and across every scale is confirmed by the table's distribution of idol-ward content across all six groups and all six periods.

**Soteriology** (the doctrine of salvation) maps to the God-ward direction of the orientation-of-pursuit dimension and the threshold-and-gateway entries. The FL.II Confession-Restoration Law's clearing function, the FL.III Heart-Throne dethroning work, the Pure-Heart Vision Law's purity-as-precondition for seeing, the Drawing-Near Reciprocity Law's reciprocity-of-response mechanism, and the cumulative additions through (Bitter-Root's reconciliation-pathway as the corrective; Household Formation's deliberate-work as the operational form of multigenerational soteriological transmission; Trust-Substrate's experienced-participation in God's character; Worship Alignment's perceptual-recalibration through worship; Soul-Restoration's God-given restoration of the soul's integration) all map to the soteriological category. The classical doctrine's claim that salvation operates as a real change in the person's orientation, with operational consequences that compound over time, is confirmed by the table's distribution of God-ward content alongside the idol-ward content.

**Pneumatology** (the doctrine of the Holy Spirit) maps primarily to Group I (Spirit) but extends to several Group V entries (Obedience Channel, Spiritual Authority, FL.XIX Spirit Anointing Transmission Law) where the Spirit's operation is the operational mechanism behind a Body/Action law. FL.XIX names the multigenerational Spirit-transmission and FL.XX names the gathered-body's Spirit-discernment, filling previously-empty cells in the Spirit column.

**Ecclesiology** (the doctrine of the church) maps to Period 3 (Community / Congregation) most directly, with extensions to Period 4 (Generational) where the multigenerational church-formation operates. The ecclesiological territory holds substantially — FL.XVIII Bitter-Root Community Law, FL.XX Gathered-Body Discernment Law, FL.XXIV Confession-in-Community Law, FL.XXV Restoration-of-the-Erring Law, FL.XXIX Corporate Emotional Integration Law, FL.XXX Communal Soul-Care for the Wounded Law, FL.XXXI Corporate Scriptural Reception Law, FL.XXXII Communal Worship Heart-Alignment Law, FL.XXXIII Community Polity Structure Law — bringing the Period 3 row to structural completion across all six Groups. The body-of-Christ master-frame provides the integrative-identity articulation within which the operational-dimension Foundational Laws operate.

**Eschatology** (the doctrine of last things) maps to Period 5 (Cosmic / Eschatological) most directly. FL.XXXVI Eschatological Glory Law holds the eschatological territory's principal articulation — the heart's sustained anticipation of the not-yet-completed glory as the catalog's eschatological-orientation foundation. The Suffering-as-Formation Loop at P5/GIII and the Attention Economy of the Soul at P5/GIV operate alongside it. The Period 5 row remains the table's most sparsely-populated territory, consistent with the broader observation that operational laws at the cosmic scale are inherently less concrete than operational laws at the more immediate scales.

The classical-doctrinal lens confirms the structural reading and provides a complementary frame for orienting readers from particular theological traditions to the table's content. A reader from the Reformed tradition can read the table through covenant theology; a reader from the Catholic and Orthodox tradition can read it through sacramental theology and patristic anthropology; a reader from the Wesleyan tradition can read it through holiness and sanctification theology. The two-axis structure is sufficiently general to accommodate the differences in framing without collapsing them.

## The Third-Axis Test and Mirror Discipline

A candidate third axis — orientation of pursuit (God-ward vs. idol-ward) — was tested and found to be real and operationally important but not warranting promotion to a third structural axis at the present density of inverse-law content. The test produced the Mirror discipline (every entry carries a Mirror field in one of three forms — Mirror statement, Constitutive marker, or Orientation-neutral marker) as the implementation of the third dimension at the entry level rather than as a structural axis.

The Mirror discipline's structural integration holds across the Foundational tier without requiring modification of the discipline's three-form structure. The constitutive-bidirectional Mirror form has proved particularly useful for the community-relational and generational-relational laws where the scriptural articulation tends to name both directions in the same passage (FL.XVIII Bitter-Root Community Law; FL.XVII Substitution-Cascade / FL.XXI Household Formation paired articulation; FL.XXII Endurance-Hope Law; FL.XXIX Corporate Emotional Integration Law).

Additional structural patterns operate alongside the Mirror discipline — the Period 0 row's proportionality pattern, the threshold/gateway pattern, the cross-Group cross-scale nearness-or-distance articulation, the two-Foundational-Laws-at-one-cell pattern (FL.XXXV and FL.XXXVII coexisting at P0/GII as mutually-reinforcing substrate mechanisms), and the substrate-to-operation relationship across distinct Groups at the same scale (the inner-Group substrates condition the outer-Group operations across the Period 0 row). These patterns operate as structural-observation sub-sections of the present chapter rather than as additional structural axes. The Group axis is not merely a categorical-classification axis but encodes operational relationships among Groups — substrate-dimensions produce operational-dimensions; orientation-dimensions condition substrate-dimensions; architectural-dimensions hold the operational-dimensions in unified form.

A future revision may revisit the structural-axis question if the catalog's growth produces a density of inverse-law content that pushes against the entry-level treatment's capacity. The present density does not approach this threshold.
