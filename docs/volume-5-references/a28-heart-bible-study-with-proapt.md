# A28 Heart Bible Study with PROAPT

## Heart Bible Study with PROAPT (V2.Exp7 — Hearing with Understanding)
## What this protocol does
PROAPT (*Pray, Read, Observe, Apply, Pray, Tell*) is the core IJH scripture-engagement practice, run in per-participant form for individual scripture-engagement or in small-group form ("Heart Bible Study") for corporate scripture-engagement. The six-step structure operationalizes the developmental claim of V2.Exp7 — *the hearing faculty grows through intentional practice across the affective taxonomy stages* — by forcing the participant through Spirit-invocation, receptive reading, observation-before-interpretation, Spirit-mediated personal application, prayer-of-yielding, and corporate-witnessed declaration of what was heard. The practice's primary downstream effect is hearing-faculty development; the secondary downstream effects are heart-soil cultivation (V2.Exp1), faith development through Romans 10:17 *hearing the word of Christ*, and obediential-formation through James 1:22 *doing the word*. In small-group form, the practice extends to corporate amplification (V2.Exp9) with the Tell step operating as corporate-witnessed declaration (V2.Exp9A).

The May 2006 Heart Bible Study document and the 2008 ASC Manual §3.1 add the group-format operational layer (three slow readings, image-and-metaphor invitation, listening for same-and-different, Gartner eight-intelligences alternative for non-metaphorical thinkers, optional Any Doubts closing). The Herman (2008) codification is the academic reference; Truro Church discipleship lineage (1980s) is the deepest historical source.

**When to use it**

• **As the ****core**** scripture-engagement practice in BoB / FotH groups.** PROAPT is the default form of group scripture-engagement; departures from it (lecture-style teaching, didactic Bible study, devotional reading) are operationally different practices serving different purposes. The Companion's discernment is which form fits which moment; for the formation work the corpus is designed for, PROAPT is the default.

• **For individual scripture-engagement** in the per-participant six-step form. The participant may run PROAPT alone or with a single accountability partner. The Tell step requires another person but does not require a full group.

• **When the participant is at affective taxonomy Stages 1–3** (Receiving / Responding / Valuing). The practice is foundational for these stages. At Stages 4–5 (Organization / Characterization), the practice remains useful but is held differently — less as a discrete protocol-event, more as an integrated rhythm of how the participant engages scripture.

• **Level I Companion competency** for leading the protocol. The structural moves (framing the six steps, holding witness-discipline during Apply and Tell, closing cleanly) are operationally simple. The Apply-step discernment-of-voices governance per V2.Exp7A is itself a competency that matures with reps; a Level I Companion can lead the protocol from this entry alone with the understanding that the Apply-step discernment will deepen over time.

**When NOT to use it (failure modes and contraindications)**

• **Treating PROAPT as a pedagogical exercise rather than as Spirit-mediated practice.** The most common failure mode. The Pray-opening becomes throat-clearing; the Apply step becomes the participant's self-application-as-authority; the Tell step gets omitted as add-on accountability rather than as constitutive declaration. The Companion's posture during the practice determines whether the practice operates in the Spirit-mediated register or in the pedagogical register; if the Companion treats the structure as a technique, the group will follow. Lead by what you are doing, not by what you are saying.

• **Skipping the Tell step.** The single most consequential failure-pattern. The existing V2.Exp7 prose names this explicitly: *"the Tell step is the one most often omitted and the one most important for long-term development."* A practice that closes at Pray-again has cut itself off from what makes it formational. Run the Tell step every time; do not let the practice complete without it.

• **Letting the Apply step drift into self-application-as-authority.** A participant who reads scripture and applies it to themselves without testing the application against scripture-elsewhere, against the Companion's discernment, and against the group's hearing is operating as their own core authority. The Companion's framing (*"What is the Spirit highlighting in this passage for you in this moment?"*) and the V2.Exp7A discernment-of-voices governance (the Apply step's impressions are tested per 1 John 4:1) are non-optional.

• **Importing inner-work protocols into PROAPT mid-practice.** A participant's Apply-step impression surfaces a wound, a sin, a shadow, or a piece of carpet-style material; the Companion or a listener turns the practice into A17 (Carpet-Style Healing and Forgiveness) or A30 (Shadow Mission) or A12 (Splitting a Role) mid-PROAPT. Category error. PROAPT stays a scripture-engagement practice; the inner-work, where indicated, comes after in its own protocol.

• **Treating PROAPT as Lectio Divina.** The two practices are adjacent but operationally different. PROAPT is corporate-obediential-applied; Lectio Divina is individual-contemplative-receptive. Importing Lectio's *Contemplatio* (resting-in-God-after-reading) into PROAPT's Pray-again step changes the protocol's purpose. The Companion's discernment is which practice fits which moment; the core IJH practice is PROAPT.

• **Running PROAPT before the participant's heart-soil is sufficiently prepared.** V2.Exp1 governs heart-soil. A participant whose heart-soil is in *the path* condition (Mark 4 sower parable) cannot receive scripture in the Apply-step sense the practice depends on. The upstream V2 explorations diagnose and address heart-soil before PROAPT will produce its full work. A man at Stage 1 affective taxonomy attending PROAPT for the first months may experience the practice as flat; the Stage 1 work is the upstream practice.

• **Pressing the Apply step when the participant is in Stage 1.** New participants often cannot produce specific Apply-step impressions for several months. The Companion's discernment: hold the practice's Pray-Read-Observe steps lightly, accept whatever Apply-step content the participant produces (even if thin or generic), and trust the practice's developmental effect over time. Pressing for "deeper" Apply-step content in a Stage 1 participant produces shame or performance, not formation.

## The protocol — per-participant six-step form
1. **Pray (opening).**

- Participant or Companion: invoke the Spirit. *"Holy Spirit, we are approaching the Word as those who expect to hear from You. Speak; we are listening."* The wording varies; the operational move is the invocation, not the specific phrasing.

- Discernment: this is operational, not ceremonial. If the prayer feels rushed or formulaic, slow it down; the channel-opening matters.

2. **Read.**

- Companion or designated reader: read the scripture passage aloud. In group form, read three times slowly with the group's eyes closed (except the reader's). In individual form, read at a pace that lets the words land.

- Participant: receive the passage. Do not analyze yet; receive.

- Discernment: if the participant immediately moves to interpretation, gently re-cue back to receiving. The receiving precedes the interpreting.

3. **Observe.**

- Companion: invite observation. *"What do you notice in this passage? What stands out? What is strange or unexpected? What do you not understand?"*

- Participant: respond from honest observation. Multiple observations are normal; some will be from immediate noticing, some will emerge as the participant sits with the passage.

- Discernment: observation is descriptive, not interpretive. If the participant moves to interpretation prematurely, gently re-cue back to observation. The honest observation precedes the interpretation.

4. **Apply.**

- Companion: invite Spirit-mediated application. *"What is the Spirit highlighting in this passage for you, today, in your specific situation?"*

- Participant: receive what the Spirit shows them; speak what they hear.

- Discernment (V2.Exp7A governs): test the impression. Is it consistent with scripture? Is it Spirit-conviction (heed and confess), accusation (resist), or internalized lie (address therapeutically)? If the application is provisional or unclear, hold it as such; do not press for certainty. The application is subject to weighing per 1 Cor 14:29.

5. **Pray (again).**

- Participant: bring the application to God. *"Lord, what I have heard is X. What I am willing to do with it is Y. What I am struggling with is Z. Help me."* The wording varies; the operational move is the prayer-of-yielding.

- Discernment: this is where the obedience channel opens. The yielding-prayer is what holds the application accountable to obedience rather than letting it evaporate.

6. **Tell.**

- Participant: speak what was heard to another person (in group form, to the group). *"What I heard from this passage is X. What I am going to do with it is Y."*

- Group (in group form): receive the telling per the Companion-as-witness discipline (Claim 10 governance, pending Council action). The telling is received as corporate-witnessed declaration; reflection, if any, is brief and held in receiving mode.

- Discernment: the Tell step is non-negotiable. If the participant resists Tell, the Companion gently presses — *"this step is the one that makes the practice what it is."* The corporate-witnessed declaration is what makes the heard word real.

**The protocol — small-group "Heart Bible Study" extension**

The group-format adds the May 2006 Heart Bible Study operational layer over the per-participant six-step structure.

1. **Container opening** per V2.Exp8 (formally marked, per Claim 5).

2. **Three slow readings of the scripture passage.** Group's eyes closed except the reader; the reader varies across the three readings if desired. The repetition lets the passage land in different registers.

3. **Image-and-metaphor invitation.** *"What images, metaphors, or feelings arose as you listened?"* Participants speak briefly; the Companion holds receiving mode. *Alternative for non-metaphorical thinkers (the Gartner alternative):* if a participant does not think in metaphors, invite engagement through another modality — music (what would this passage sound like?), drawing (what would it look like?), sculpture (what would it feel like in your hands?), dance (what would it move like?), acting (what would it be like to embody it?). The Companion's discernment is which door fits which participant.

4. **Same-and-different reflection.** *"What did you hear that was the same as what others heard? What was different?"* The same-and-different reflection often produces the moment where the Spirit shows the group something none of them individually heard.

5. **Observe step** (per the per-participant structure, individually or with group input).

6. **Apply step** (per the per-participant structure). In group form, each participant may share their Apply-step impression; the group holds receiving mode while each speaks.

7. **Pray-again step** (per the per-participant structure). Often done individually in silence with the Companion's spoken framing.

8. **Tell step** (per the per-participant structure, with the corporate-witnessed pattern in group form). Each participant may briefly speak what they heard and what they will do with it; the group receives as witness.

9. **Optional Any Doubts closing.** When a participant has heard a specific truth in Apply but is not yet fully trusting it, the A29 Any Doubts protocol can be run on the heard truth before the session closes. Cross-reference to *Vol5_Part_VI_A29_AnyDoubts.md* for the operational protocol; the Companion may run the close-form Any Doubts (which is shorter than the full A29 protocol) as the natural transition.

10. **Container close** per V2.Exp8 (formally marked), with V2.Exp9A corporate-witness affirmation pattern applied if appropriate (the group may affirm what the Lord has done in each participant's hearing tonight, in brief).

**Closing and de-roling (if applicable)**

**Not applicable.** PROAPT does not involve role-play; no figures are externalized; no participant holds a role for another. The de-roling protocol per A14 / refined V2.Exp8 is not invoked. The container closing uses the standard V2.Exp8 marked-closing form.

## Companion notes
The Apply step is where to put your attention as you grow into leading PROAPT. The other five steps are structurally simple; a Companion can do them adequately after a few reps. What separates a PROAPT session that does its work from one that produces mere intellectual engagement is whether the Apply step operates in the Spirit-mediated register (the Spirit's application of scripture-already-given to this participant in this moment) or in the pedagogical register (the participant's interpretive application of the text to their life). The framing language matters — *"What is the Spirit highlighting in this passage for you in this moment?"* — and your own posture matters more. If you are in receiving mode during Apply, the participant typically receives in that register too. If you drift into evaluative-listening or interpretive-analysis, the participant follows.

The Tell step is the second place to put your attention. Companions facilitating PROAPT often let the practice complete at Pray-again because Tell feels optional or because the participant's resistance to Tell creates social friction the Companion does not want to manage. Hold the Tell step as non-negotiable. The 1 Timothy 6:12 *good confession before many witnesses* is what the step operationalizes; without it, the practice ends as private affair rather than corporate formation.

A pacing note. PROAPT in group format typically runs 60–90 minutes for a single passage with full six-step engagement plus the May 2006 group-format layer. Compressing it into 30 minutes truncates the meditation-and-application work the practice depends on; extending past 90 minutes typically produces diminishing returns. The Companion's discernment determines the right pacing for the group; the operational default is single passage per session at 60–90 minutes.

A note on the Gartner alternative (Founder-attention flag 2 in the Phase 2 entry, pending decision). The eight-intelligences alternative is operationally well-formed and serves participants who do not think metaphorically. When you have a non-metaphorical thinker in your group, the alternative is the right move; do not press image-and-metaphor work on someone for whom it does not land. The framework structure (Spirit-invocation, receptive engagement, observation, Spirit-application, prayer-of-yielding, corporate-witnessed declaration) stays the same; the door is what varies.

A final note on the developmental scope. PROAPT is foundational for affective taxonomy Stages 1–3. At Stages 4–5, the practice is held differently — less as a discrete protocol-event, more as the integrated rhythm of how the participant engages scripture across daily life. A mature participant who has been doing PROAPT for years may not run all six steps formally in every engagement; the structure has internalized, and the rhythm operates without the protocol scaffolding. Companions facilitating mixed-stage groups should expect this difference and hold space for both forms — the explicit-protocol form for newer participants, the internalized-rhythm form for mature participants doing the practice alongside.
